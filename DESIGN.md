# Hybrid Track Design Direction

Product: Hybrid Track
Audience: people managing hybrid workflows, schedules, progress, or tracking.
Personality: clear, practical, modern, trustworthy.
UI mood: focused dashboard, readable, efficient, not flashy.
Copy tone: direct, human, useful, not marketing-heavy.
Avoid: generic AI landing page style, fake stats, vague claims, dead buttons, decorative-only cards.
Priority: mobile-friendly, accessible, fast to scan, real usable flows.
